This Cookie Policy explains how AfroStory uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website and related services. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service .

01. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar identifiers that websites and online services may store on a user's device or access through the user's browser. They can help a website remember information, maintain sessions, provide security, understand how the service is used, and support advertising or other functionality.

AfroStory may also use technologies that perform functions similar to cookies, including browser storage, pixels, scripts, tags and other technical mechanisms. In this policy, we refer to these technologies collectively as "cookies and similar technologies."

02. Why AfroStory Uses Cookies

Depending on the feature being used and the technologies enabled on the platform, AfroStory may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

Strictly Necessary Technologies

These technologies are used to provide essential functions of AfroStory, including authentication, maintaining a user's session, security, fraud prevention, account protection and other functions necessary for the operation of the platform.

These technologies may be required for the website or particular features to function correctly. Disabling them may cause authentication, account or security-related functionality to stop working.

Authentication and Account Sessions

AfroStory may use cookies or other browser storage mechanisms to maintain authenticated sessions and help keep users signed in securely.

The exact names and technical implementation of authentication cookies may change as the platform is updated. We do not rely on a particular cookie name as a permanent description of our authentication system.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, Google may use cookies and other technologies as part of its authentication and security processes. These technologies are controlled by Google, not by AfroStory.

Information received by AfroStory through Google authentication is handled according to our Privacy Policy .

GitHub Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using GitHub, GitHub may use cookies and similar technologies as part of its authentication, security and account-management processes. These technologies are controlled by GitHub, not by AfroStory.

Where enabled, AfroStory may use analytics technologies such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the platform, identify performance problems, measure engagement and improve features and content.

These services may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as advertising delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, advertising personalization.

AfroStory is operated in Nigeria and seeks to handle personal data in accordance with applicable Nigerian data protection requirements, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDP Act) , together with other applicable legal requirements.
