Your Story, Your Platform

AfroStory is a digital streaming platform dedicated entirely to authentic African narratives. We believe that the stories of the continent deserve a global stage, untainted by stereotypes.

From urban thrillers to rich traditional folklore and insightful documentaries, AfroStory connects you directly with talented African creators. Watch original series, discover new filmmakers, and explore a growing library of continental cinema.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AfroStory?

AfroStory is a digital streaming platform focused on delivering authentic African movies, series, documentaries, and short films directly to a global audience.

How do I become a creator?

Create a free user account, navigate to the Studio section, and submit a creator application for our admins to review.

Where do the stories come from?

Our content library is built by passionate independent filmmakers, storytellers, and production studios from across the African continent.
