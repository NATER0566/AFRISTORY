Your Story, Your Platform

AfroStory is the definitive premium digital streaming platform dedicated entirely to authentic African narratives. We believe that the stories of the continent deserve a global stage, untainted by stereotypes and delivered with the highest production quality.

Whether you are looking for heart-pounding urban thrillers, rich traditional folklore, insightful documentaries, or visionary modern Afro-futurism, AfroStory connects you directly with the continent's most talented creators. By watching on AfroStory, you aren't just consuming content; you are directly supporting the African film ecosystem.

Our unique model empowers creators to monetize their art while giving viewers flexible access. Join us today and discover a world of stories made here, for everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AfroStory?

AfroStory is a premium digital streaming platform focused on delivering authentic African movies, series, documentaries, and short films directly to a global audience.

Is AfroStory free to use?

Yes! You can create a free account and watch a wide selection of ad-supported content directly from independent African creators.

How do creators earn money?

Creators upload their original series to the Creator Studio. The platform helps independent African filmmakers and studios monetize their art directly through our viewing ecosystem.

Can I watch on my mobile phone?

Absolutely. AfroStory is fully optimized for mobile streaming, ensuring fast load times and a beautiful vertical feed experience for series discovery.

How do I become a creator?

Simply create a free user account, navigate to the Studio section, and submit a creator application. Once our admins approve your profile, you can start publishing immediately.

Where do the stories come from?

Our content library is built by passionate independent filmmakers, storytellers, and production studios from across the entire African continent.
