About Our Platform

Your Story, Your Platform

AfriStory is a digital streaming platform dedicated to authentic African narratives—connecting audiences worldwide with original serialized dramas, cultural epics, short films, and documentaries created by African storytellers.

Our Mission

African cinema and storytelling carry centuries of rich oral history, contemporary urban energy, and diverse cultural perspectives. AfriStory exists to give these stories a dedicated global stage without creative compromise.

We champion productions across indigenous and Pan-African languages—including Tiv, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Swahili, and English—so viewers can experience stories told in their authentic voices.

What You Can Watch

Our catalog is built for serialized binge-watching and discovery across multiple genres and formats:

  • Serialized original dramas and episodic cliffhangers
  • Traditional folklore, historical epics, and cultural heritage films
  • Independent African short films and emerging director showcases
  • Documentaries exploring music, history, and modern African life

Empowering African Creators

Through the AfriStory Creator Studio, independent filmmakers, writers, and production teams can publish series directly to a dedicated streaming audience.

Creators retain ownership of their creative vision while accessing audience analytics, community engagement tools, and transparent monetization opportunities tied to viewership.

Accessible Viewing & Rewards

Anyone can register for a free AfriStory account to explore previews, track watch history, and follow favorite creators.

Viewers can earn free daily Story Coins through check-ins and ad-supported rewards to unlock episodes, or choose VIP access for uninterrupted streaming across devices.

Platform Ownership & Editorial Contact

AfriStory operates officially at https://afristory.live. We review creator submissions for originality, copyright compliance, and community safety standards in accordance with our Terms of Service and DMCA Policy.

For partnership inquiries, press requests, creator onboarding, or viewer support, contact our team directly at support@afristory.live or visit our Contact Us page.
