Copyright Protection

DMCA & Copyright Policy

AfriStory respects the intellectual property rights of creators, copyright owners, and other rights holders. This policy explains how copyright concerns can be reported to AfriStory.

1. Overview

AfriStory is a digital storytelling and entertainment platform that allows creators and users to upload, publish, discover, and watch digital content.

We respect the rights of copyright owners and expect everyone using AfriStory to respect applicable copyright laws.

If you believe that content available through AfriStory infringes your copyright, you may submit a copyright infringement notice using the contact information provided below.

2. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative of a copyright owner, you may contact AfriStory regarding content that you believe infringes your copyright.

To help us investigate your complaint, your notice should contain sufficient information to identify the copyrighted work and the material that you believe infringes it.

Information to Include

  1. Your full name and contact information.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed.
  3. A description of the material that you believe infringes your copyright.
  4. The URL or other specific location where the allegedly infringing content can be found on AfriStory.
  5. A statement explaining your relationship to the copyrighted work or your authority to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  6. Any additional information that may help us locate and review the reported content.

3. Copyright Complaint Statement

Your complaint should accurately identify the material at issue and should be submitted in good faith.

Important: Please do not submit false, misleading, or fraudulent copyright complaints. Misuse of the copyright reporting process may have legal consequences.

4. What Happens After a Report

After receiving a copyright complaint, AfriStory may review the information provided and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.

Depending on the situation, actions may include restricting access to the reported material, contacting the uploader, requesting additional information, or taking other measures permitted by applicable law.

Submitting a complaint does not automatically guarantee that content will be removed. We may request additional information where necessary to evaluate a report.

5. Counter-Notification

If your content has been restricted or removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe the action was made in error, you may contact AfriStory and explain why you believe the restriction should be reconsidered.

Your request should identify the affected content and provide sufficient information for us to understand the basis of your dispute.

We may request additional information or documentation before restoring content.

6. Repeat Copyright Infringement

AfriStory may take appropriate action against accounts that repeatedly upload or distribute content that infringes the rights of others.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include restricting content, suspending account functionality, or terminating an account where appropriate.

7. Creator Responsibility

Creators are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights, permissions, licenses, or other lawful authorization to upload and distribute the content they publish on AfriStory.

Creators should not upload copyrighted material belonging to another person or organization unless they have the necessary authorization or a lawful basis for doing so.
