Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

We use cookies behind the scenes to keep you logged in, secure our platform, and serve ads. No separate popup is shown at this time — by using AfroStory you consent to the cookies described below.

01. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use cookies and similar storage (local storage) to operate login, remember sessions, prevent fraud, and deliver advertising. Some are set by us, some by third parties you interact with (Google, GitHub).

02. How We Use Cookies (No Popup)

AfroStory currently sets cookies automatically when you use the site. There is no accept/reject banner. Continued use of the site constitutes consent. You can block or delete cookies anytime in your browser settings (see section 04).

Strictly Necessary — Our Code

Set automatically by AfroStory to make login work. The site will break without these.

afrostory_token — JWT session, keeps you logged in
afrostory_refresh — Refreshes your session securely
csrf_token — Security against form attacks

Third-Party Login Cookies

When you choose "Login with Google" or "Login with GitHub", those providers set their own cookies to verify your identity:

  • Google OAuth: accounts.google.com sets authentication cookies to confirm your Google account. Governed by Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub OAuth: github.com sets authentication cookies to confirm your GitHub account. Governed by GitHub Privacy Statement.

We only receive your name, email, and avatar from them — we do not control their cookies.

Advertising Cookies

Google Ad Manager / AdSense automatically sets cookies including DoubleClick to personalize ads.

Opt out of Google Ads Learn More

03. Your Choices

Since we don't show a banner, your browser is your control panel. You can delete existing cookies and block future ones, but blocking necessary cookies will log you out and break Google/GitHub login.

ChromeSettings → Privacy and security → Cookies
SafariSettings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
FirefoxSettings → Privacy & Security → Cookies

Recommendation

To improve compliance with NDPR/GDPR, we plan to add a cookie consent banner in future that will let you accept/reject non-essential cookies. This policy will be updated when that launches.
