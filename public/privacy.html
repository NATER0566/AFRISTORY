01. Introduction

Welcome to AfroStory. We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when it may be shared with service providers and advertising partners, and the choices and rights available to you.

AfroStory is operated from Nigeria and processes personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), where applicable, and other privacy laws that may apply based on the location and circumstances of a user.

Where additional legal requirements apply to users in other jurisdictions, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or applicable jurisdictions in the United States, AfroStory will apply the requirements applicable to the relevant processing activities.

02. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an AfroStory account, we may collect information such as your username, email address, authentication credentials, profile information, profile picture or avatar, account preferences, and information necessary to operate your account.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in or register using Google, AfroStory may receive information provided through the Google authentication process, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier, subject to the permissions and information made available by Google.

GitHub Sign-In

If you choose to authenticate through GitHub, AfroStory may receive information made available through the GitHub authentication process, such as your GitHub account identifier, username, email address, or profile information, depending on the permissions granted and information provided by GitHub.

Creator and Uploaded Content Information

If you use AfroStory's creator features, we may collect information associated with content you upload or publish, including video or media files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, categories, creator profile information, publication information, and related metadata.

Streaming and Activity Information

When you use AfroStory, we may process information about videos or episodes you watch, continue watching, favorite, search for, unlock, or otherwise interact with, together with relevant timestamps and account activity.

Rewards and Unlock Information

Where AfroStory provides rewards, advertising-based unlocks, coins, episode access, or related features, we may record information necessary to administer those features, including reward claims, unlocks, transaction records, and related account activity.

Technical and Security Information

Depending on how you access AfroStory, we may process information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location derived from technical information where applicable, pages or features accessed, timestamps, referring information, error information, and security-related logs.

Essential cookies or similar technologies may be used for authentication, session management, security, account functionality, and other functions necessary for the operation of AfroStory. Optional Cookies and Technologies

Depending on your location, preferences, and the services enabled on AfroStory, optional technologies may be used for analytics, advertising, advertising measurement, personalization, or similar purposes.

Cookie Choices

Third-party services used on AfroStory may place or read cookies or use similar technologies on a user's browser. These services may include advertising, analytics, authentication, and other technology providers.

Rocket Sloth Rewarded Advertising

AfroStory uses Rocket Sloth rewarded advertising features to provide advertising-supported episode unlocking and reward experiences. When a user chooses to participate in a rewarded advertisement, Rocket Sloth may process information through its advertising technology to deliver, measure, or validate the rewarded advertising experience, subject to Rocket Sloth's own policies and applicable law.

AfroStory receives the rewarded-ad result needed to determine whether the relevant rewarded action was completed. AfroStory uses that result to request server-side validation of the corresponding episode unlock or reward before recording the entitlement in its own systems.

Advertising Partners

Additional advertising or monetization providers may be added to AfroStory from time to time. Where required, this Privacy Policy and the relevant consent mechanisms will be updated to reflect material changes in the services or technologies used.
